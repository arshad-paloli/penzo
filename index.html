Penzo Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Summary

Penzo keeps everything you enter on your phone. There is no Penzo account, no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

What Penzo stores

Your expenses, income, budgets, savings, lent and borrowed records, habits, to-dos, goals, settings and an optional profile name and picture. All of it is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone.

Internet use

The app uses the internet for two small things only: (1) the currency converter downloads public exchange rates from open.er-api.com, and (2) bank logos in the savings screen are fetched from Google's public favicon service, which receives only the bank's website name. None of your financial data is sent in either request.

Permissions

Notifications are used only to show the reminders you set. Penzo does not draw over other apps. Biometric or device lock is used only for the optional app lock; Penzo never receives your fingerprint or face data. Photos or files are accessed only when you choose one (profile picture, feedback pictures, backup files).

Backups and exports

Backups, CSV files and PDF statements are created only when you ask. You choose where they are saved or shared. Anyone who gets the file can read it, so keep it safe. If you turn on Google Drive backup, you sign in with your Google account and Penzo saves the backup file to a private app folder in your own Google Drive, which only Penzo can open. Penzo never sees your Google password, uses only the drive.appdata permission, and keeps nothing on any server of ours. You can disconnect at any time in More > Data.

Feedback

When you send feedback, Penzo opens your email app with a message addressed to penzoapp@gmail.com. Pictures and the app log are attached only if you choose them. The log has error and start-up details, no expenses. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your email app.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Penzo deletes all data stored by the app. Back up first if you want to keep it, because we cannot recover it for you.

Children

Penzo is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and on its Play Store page.
